Dude Cup

Tiny top-down soccer. Push, kick, score.

← Back to the club

Controls

One player: move with WASD or the arrow keys, kick with Space (or X or Shift).
Two players, one keyboard: left player uses WASD and Space, right player uses the arrow keys and Enter.
Phone: use the joystick and the KICK button (one player modes).

Kick when you're touching the ball. Release the kick key before you can kick again. Tied at time up? The next goal wins.
