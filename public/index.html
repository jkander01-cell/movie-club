How the club works
  1. Suggest. Everyone adds movies they want to watch, up to 2 at a time. The organizer draws one at random the week before each screening and emails everyone.
  2. Watch together. We watch on the 1st and 3rd Sunday of every month, at the same time. The date and place are on the Now Showing card.
  3. Can't make it? Watch it on your own at home before we discuss, and fill out the survey ahead of time.
  4. Fill out the survey. After watching, everyone answers the After the Credits questions on their own, without peeking at anyone else's.
  5. Discuss. Once you've saved your answers, everyone else's unlock. Compare them together.

How the draw works

The draw is random but fair. Every member gets the same chance no matter how many movies they've entered, members who haven't had a pick in a while get better odds, and movies lots of people have already seen are less likely to come up.
